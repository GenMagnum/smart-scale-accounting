A simple, transparent approach

Your inputs stay with you.

The workload check, calculator, pilot planner and enquiry planner run in your browser. Smart Scale Accounting does not receive or save the information you enter on this site. Your entries clear when the page is refreshed or closed. The optional app caches public website files on your device for offline use; it does not save your answers or enquiry details between sessions. Your browser controls retention and removal of cached files.

Copying or downloading a brief creates a copy under your control. Email links open your email app with a draft for you to review and send. When you send an email to smartscale2026@gmail.com, Smart Scale Accounting receives that message through its email provider. You decide what to share. Please use sample volumes and workflow descriptions rather than client names, account numbers or confidential financial records.

Before any work starts, access permissions, client consent where needed, confidentiality and data-handling arrangements must be agreed with your practice. Hosting access and technical logs are managed by the hosting provider.
